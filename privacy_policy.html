Privacy Policy

Chicago RP Staff Application Form · Effective date: August 14, 2026

1. Who we are

Chicago RP ("we", "us") operates the Chicago RP Staff Application Form. This page explains how we collect, use and protect your information when you submit an application.

2. Information we collect

When you fill in and submit the staff application form, we collect:

  • Your answers to the form questions, including your Discord username, Discord user ID, Roblox username, age, country, moderation experience and activity level.
  • Your public Discord profile information (display name and avatar) and your public Roblox profile information (display name and avatar), which the form fetches automatically in your browser to verify that the account belongs to you.
  • The country you select and its time zone.
  • Your IP address (attached to the submission email to help prevent duplicate or abusive applications).
  • The date and time of your submission.

3. How we use your information

We use this information solely to review staff applications, to verify your Discord and Roblox accounts, to prevent duplicate or abusive submissions, and to contact you if your application moves forward.

To verify your Discord and Roblox accounts, we query the public Discord and Roblox APIs. Only your public profile (display name and avatar) is retrieved; we never access passwords or private messages.

4. Storage and retention

  • Your answers are stored securely on our server and are kept for as long as needed to review applications.
  • Your IP address is stored temporarily and is automatically removed from our records 7 days (1 week) after submission.
  • To prevent duplicate applications, only one response is accepted from the same IP address within a 48-hour (2-day) window. After that window you may submit again.

5. Email notifications

Your application is sent by automated email to the Chicago RP staff team so that it can be reviewed.

6. Local saving on your device

While you fill in the form, your progress is saved automatically in your browser's local storage so that you do not lose your answers if you close the page. Your answers are not sent to us until you press "Submit". The only exception is account verification: as soon as you enter your Discord user ID or Roblox username, we look up your public profile to show you a confirmation prompt. That lookup is not stored until you submit.

7. What you should not submit

Please do not include passwords or any sensitive or confidential information in your application. We cannot guarantee the security of information you choose to share in free-text answers.

8. Your rights

You can contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your application data. If you ask us to delete your data, we will remove it as soon as we reasonably can.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, you can reach the Chicago RP staff team at any time by opening a support ticket in our Discord server, or by messaging the user @n3tdream on Discord.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
